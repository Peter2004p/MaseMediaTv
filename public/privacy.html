Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MaseNet handles information when you use our website, video library, account sign-in, AI assistant, or Telegram experiences.

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Important: This is a general policy for the features currently intended for MaseNet. Actual data practices depend on which services and integrations are enabled. Please contact the site operator for questions or deletion requests.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details: information provided by sign-in services, such as your name, email address, profile image, and provider account identifier, when made available to MaseNet.
  • Usage and technical data: pages visited, searches, titles opened, timestamps, browser/device information, approximate network information in hosting logs, and security or error events.
  • AI conversations: messages and details you choose to send to the MaseNet AI assistant, plus information needed to provide its responses.
  • Telegram data: when you use the bot or Mini App, Telegram may provide account identifiers, username/display information, and message or interaction content needed to respond.
  • Saved preferences: interface settings and playback or watch-list information that may be stored in your browser or account.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to sign you in, maintain sessions, show the catalogue and playback features, remember preferences, answer AI requests, operate Telegram features, understand aggregate usage, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, protect the service, and respond to support or privacy requests.

3. Cookies and local storage

MaseNet may use browser storage and session technologies to remember preferences, authentication state, watch progress, and saved items. Your browser settings can clear or restrict local storage, but doing so may sign you out or reset preferences. Essential storage may be needed for the site to work.

4. Service providers and sharing

MaseNet may rely on service providers to host the site and data, provide authentication, deliver AI responses, or operate Telegram integration. Depending on the feature used, relevant information may be processed by Cloudflare, Google, OpenAI, Telegram, or other configured providers under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information to advertisers. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or its security.

5. AI assistant

When you send a message to the AI assistant, the message and necessary context may be sent to the configured AI service to generate a response. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, sensitive personal information, or anything you would not want processed by the service providers. AI responses can be inaccurate and should not be treated as professional advice.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including account operation, service reliability, security, and legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by provider and data type. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the service, sign out, clear browser storage, or change available browser permissions. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting the site operator. We may need to verify your request and retain limited records where legally required or necessary for security. Deleting browser data does not necessarily delete server-side or provider-held records.

8. Children

MaseNet is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the site operator so the issue can be reviewed.

9. External links and third-party policies

The service may link to external websites, video hosts, Google, or Telegram. Those services operate under their own policies, and MaseNet does not control their practices. Review their policies before using their services.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when MaseNet features or data practices change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, access/correction requests, or deletion requests, contact the MaseNet site operator through the official support/contact method provided on the MaseNet website or its official Telegram presence. Include enough information for us to locate the relevant account, but do not send your password.
