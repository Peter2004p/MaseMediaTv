Supported formats & honest limits

MP4 (H.264/AAC) and WebM are the safest formats for browser playback. HLS (.m3u8) is supported when the stream is accessible; some browsers play it natively, while others use HLS.js. MKV support depends on browser/codec and is not reliable in standard HTML video. A link that has expired, requires login, or is blocked by its host cannot be fixed by the player alone.
